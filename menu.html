Daily Flour, Water, Salt & Time

The Daily Hearth Rotation

Every loaf we shape undergoes a minimum forty-eight hour cold fermentation process. This slow breakdown of starches yields a complex organic profile, a highly digestible crumb, and our signature blistered crust.

We bake daily in small, scientifically measured batches. Our schedule honors the natural lifecycle of wild yeast cultures, shifting slightly with the barometric pressure and ambient humidity of our Portland workshop.

Heritage Country Sourdough

Our signature bake. Crafted from seventy-five percent locally milled organic hard red wheat and twenty-five percent raw rye flour. It yields an open, gelatinized crumb structure, a deep caramelized mahogany crust, and a sharp lactic acidity that lingers gracefully on the palate.

Laminated Mastery

81-Layer Croissant

Our viennoiserie is a testament to rigorous thermal management. We laminate premium grass-fed cultured butter into an enriched organic flour dough through exactly three single-letter folds. The result is a highly defined geometric honeycomb spiral that shatters effortlessly upon first bite.
